# How to play Euchre

> Every rule here comes from src/shared/games/euchre/engine.ts (EUCHRE_DEFAULTS: first to 10, stick the dealer on).

## teams {chapter="Teams and cards"}
Euchre is for four players, in [#teams] two teams of two. [#partner] Your partner sits across from you. Here the teams are [#deer] Deer Camp and [#lake] Lake Effect.

## deck
It uses [#deck] {24|twenty-four} cards, [#ranks] nine through Ace in each suit. [#deal] Everyone gets five, and [#up] one is turned up.

## round1 {chapter="Picking trump" transition=push}
Next you pick [#trump] trump, the suit that beats the others. [#left] Starting left of the dealer, each player can [#order] order up that card's suit, or [#pass] pass. [#pickup] If it's ordered up, the dealer takes the card and throws one away.

## round2 {transition=push}
If everyone passes, [#down] it's turned down, and players can [#name] name another suit. [#stick] The dealer can't pass again. [#alone] Whoever names trump can go alone, and their partner sits out.

## bowers {chapter="The trump order" transition=push}
[#right] The Jack of trump is the highest card, the right bower. [#left] Next is the left bower, the other Jack of the same color, [#counts] which counts as trump. [#rest] Then Ace, King, Queen, ten and nine.

## tricks {chapter="Playing tricks" transition=push}
Left of the dealer [#lead] leads first. [#follow] Follow the suit that was led if you can. [#trumpwins] The highest trump wins the trick, [#highest] or else the highest card of the led suit. [#next] The winner leads next.

## scoring {chapter="Scoring" transition=push}
The team that named trump needs [#three] three tricks. [#one] Three or four score one point. [#five] All five score two, [#alone] or four alone. [#euchred] Fewer than three, they're euchred, and the other team scores two. [#ten] First to ten wins.

## app {chapter="Playing on the TV"}
Here, [#tv] the table is on the TV, and [#phone] your cards are on your phone, [#buttons] with order up and pass right by your hand.

## summary {transition=push}
[#s1] Pick trump, [#s2] follow suit, and [#s3] take three tricks with your partner.
